## Response Options

All questions except the previous programming experience question used the following seven-point Likert scale:

1. Strongly Disagree
2. Disagree
3. Somewhat Disagree
4. Neutral
5. Somewhat Agree
6. Agree
7. Strongly Agree

The previous programming experience question used the following response options:

1. Formal coursework, such as high school or other college coursework
2. Self-taught, such as through online tutorials or personal projects
3. Both formal coursework and self-taught experience
4. No prior experience
